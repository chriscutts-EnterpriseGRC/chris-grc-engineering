# Assess — ISO/IEC 42001
Maps current UCF AI controls to 42001 clauses and Annex A, scores coverage across all 140 controls, and prioritises gaps.
## Arguments
- `$1` - Scope: all | clause4 | clause5 | clause6 | clause7 | clause8 | clause9 | clause10 | annexa (default: all)
## Output
Clause-by-clause coverage table, gap list sorted by risk impact, and crosswalk to NIST AI RMF and EU AI Act obligations.

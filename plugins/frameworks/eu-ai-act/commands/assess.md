# Assess — EU AI Act
Classifies AI system risk tier, maps Article obligations, and scores compliance against current UCF controls.
## Arguments
- `$1` - AI system name or description (required)
- `$2` - Suspected tier: prohibited | high-risk | limited | minimal (optional, agent will infer)
## Output
Risk tier classification with justification, Article obligation checklist, gap summary, enforcement deadline, and recommended remediation plan.

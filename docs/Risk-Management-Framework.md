# 1.  Purpose
This document establishes the Security Risk Program's comprehensive risk management framework, providing a clear, structured, and repeatable process for identifying, assessing, treating, monitoring, and reporting risks. By integrating best practices aligned with ISO 31000 and ISO 27005, it enables the organization to:
Guides systematic risk identification, analysis, evaluation, and treatment
Ensures alignment with strategic objectives and regulatory requirements
Supports proactive risk management and safeguards critical assets
Maintains a consistent and up-to-date risk register
Enables informed decision-making and effective stakeholder communication
Fosters a culture of continuous improvement across the organization


# 2.  Scope
This framework applies to all business units, teams, and systems within the organization's security risk program scope.

# 5.  Risk Assessment Process
Risk assessment encompasses risk identification, analysis, and evaluation. This process is performed:
At least annually
Upon significant changes to systems, processes, or business operations
After a major incident involving mission-critical systems

### 5.2  Risk Identification
Process Steps
Understand Assets – Identify the assets, processes, and services requiring protection
Identify Threats – Identify internal and external threat sources that could exploit vulnerabilities
Analyze Vulnerabilities – Analyze systems, processes, and environments to uncover weaknesses
Define Risk Scenarios – Combine asset, threat, and vulnerability information to define risk scenarios

Identification Techniques

Threat modelling workshops
Single-point-of-failure analysis

Security tooling outputs

### 5.3  Risk Analysis

Risk Analysis Factors
Likelihood of events and their consequences

Control effectiveness
Confidence levels

Impact Scoring

Likelihood Scoring

# 7.  Risk Register Management
The risk register provides a structured process for recording, updating, monitoring, and reporting risk information to ensure accuracy and alignment with risk management strategy.
### Risk Intake
Gather risk details from your sources – audits, threat intel, PIRs, tooling, etc.
Validate, categorize, and enter data into the risk register tool
Assign risks to relevant stakeholders for analysis and treatment

### Risk Review and Update Process
Risk owners review their risks per the monitoring cadence
Update status, mitigation progress, and changes in severity or impact
Risk management team checks register fields for accuracy quarterly
Missed review enforcement: a risk that passes its review due date without an owner update is flagged in the monthly review. If unresolved within one additional cycle, the risk is escalated to the Risk Owner's manager.

### Risk Closure Criteria
Risk marked as closed when treatment measures are successfully implemented and verified
Treatment results documented, including residual risks
Evidence collected demonstrating controls reduced risk to acceptable level

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Risk | The potential for loss or damage when a threat exploits a vulnerability, considering both likelihood and impact |
| Threat | A circumstance or event with the potential to cause harm to operations, assets, or individuals |
| Issue | A problem that may not be a vulnerability itself, but could lead to one |
| Vulnerability | A weakness or flaw in a system, application, or process that could be exploited |
| Inherent Risk | The level of risk before any controls or mitigation measures are applied |
| Residual Risk | The risk that remains after safeguards have been implemented |
| Risk Appetite | The amount and type of risk an organization is willing to pursue or retain |
| Risk Tolerance | The organization's readiness to bear risk after treatment in order to achieve its objectives |
| Control | A measure that maintains and/or modifies risk |
| Treatment | Process to modify risk through selection and implementation of appropriate options |
| Asset | An item, tangible or intangible, that if compromised would be damaging to the organization |

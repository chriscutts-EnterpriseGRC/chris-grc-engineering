Calculation Formula

Health Score = (Risk × 0.40) + (Compliance × 0.30) + (Maturity × 0.20) + (Security × 0.10)

The overall health score is a weighted average of four key components. Each component is scored on a scale of 0-100, with higher scores indicating better performance. The weights reflect the relative importance of each component to overall GRC health.
